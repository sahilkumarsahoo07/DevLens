# Privacy Policy for DevLens — Developer Tools & HD Video Recorder

**Effective Date:** August 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 19, 2026  

---

### 🛡️ Overview

DevLens ("we", "our", or "the extension") is committed to respecting your privacy. DevLens is built as a privacy-first browser extension for web developers, designers, and QA testers. 

**Summary:** DevLens does **NOT** collect, store, transmit, track, or sell any personal data, browsing history, or recorded media. All feature processing, media recording, and inspections occur 100% locally within your browser.

---

### 📦 Information We Do NOT Collect

- **No Personal Identification:** We do not collect names, email addresses, IP addresses, or device identifiers.
- **No Browsing Tracking:** We do not track websites you visit, URLs, or browsing habits.
- **No Server Uploads:** Recorded screen videos, screenshot captures, color swatches, font analyses, and storage logs are processed locally and saved directly to your device. No data is sent to external servers.
- **No Telemetry or Analytics:** We do not use third-party tracking scripts, analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics), or advertising SDKs.

---

### 🔑 Permissions & How They Are Used

DevLens requests specific browser permissions strictly to perform its core functions. Here is how each permission is used:

1. **`activeTab` / `scripting`**  
   Used solely to render the inspection toolbar, box model overlays, color picker, and viewport simulator on your currently active tab when you trigger the extension.

2. **`desktopCapture` / `displayCapture`**  
   Used exclusively to capture browser tab, window, or screen video streams when you choose to record your screen. Recorded video files are processed in your browser memory and saved to your local device.

3. **`storage`**  
   Used to save your local UI preferences (such as Light/Dark theme selection and audio toggle defaults) inside Chrome's local storage API (`chrome.storage.local`).

4. **`downloads`**  
   Used to save your recorded video files (`.webm` / `.mp4`) directly to your device's default Downloads folder when you click the Download button.
